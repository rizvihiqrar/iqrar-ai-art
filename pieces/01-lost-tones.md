# Lost Tones

| At a Glance | |
|---|---|
| What | A quiet gallery wall of eighty-four made-up phones, each with its own ringtone |
| Made | 2026 |
| Best on | A desktop browser, with the sound on |
| Made by | Concept and direction by Iqrar Rizvi, code and sound by Claude |

## The Idea

*When old phones desire to hum and live.* The wall runs from a 1983 brick to the folding phones of today. None of them is a real model: each one is made up, with a ringtone of its own.

## How To Play

- **Tap a phone** to charge it, and it rings.
- **Every two taps** gives it one more round.
- **Tap its small bar** to learn about that phone.

**[Open Lost Tones](https://rizvihiqrar.github.io/iqrar-ai-art/lost-tones/)** in a desktop browser, with the sound on.

## How It Was Made

One page of plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with nothing to install. The sounds are made in the browser as you play. AI-assisted and AI-generated art.
